# Deep Learning and Neural Networks

## Published scope

Official module link: https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/executive-education/course/sctp-deep-learning-and-neural-networks/

Published classroom days: 1. The week below is an original expanded self-study schedule, not the official timetable.

## Learning outcomes
- Train a small network with backpropagation
- Inspect convolution and recurrent state updates
- Explain attention weights and distinguish a toy from a pretrained Transformer

## Five-day learning plan
- Day 1: tensors, activations and gradients (3h)
- Day 2: MLP training and failure modes (3h)
- Day 3: convolution and transfer learning (3h)
- Day 4: RNN state and attention (3h)
- Day 5: model comparison and report (4h)

## Project
A trained XOR network plus a documented sequence/vision experiment

## Boundaries
The browser core teaches mechanisms; build_lab.py actually trains small CNN, RNN and Transformer models on synthetic tasks. Pretrained models, natural-language/image benchmarks and full-scale training are not bundled.

## Completion evidence
- Clean notebook execution and changed-control screenshots
- Exercise code and deterministic tests
- Lab log with at least two failures
- Dataset/model card where appropriate
- Assessment answers before reading the solutions
